Why Do Clouds Look Fluffy?

Cotton castles. “Why fluffy?”

Many fair-weather clouds are heaps of tiny water droplets or ice crystals clustered where rising moist air cools. Sunlight scatters from countless droplets, making bright white puff shapes.

Clouds can also look flat or streaky depending on winds aloft. Fluffy is just one style.

So fluff is light bouncing off a crowd of tiny droplets.
