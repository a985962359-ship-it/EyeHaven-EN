Why Do Flowers Smell Nice?

A garden blossom smells sweet today.

Many flowers make scents to invite helpers.

Bees and butterflies follow smell and color.

Helpers move pollen from flower to flower.

Pollen travel helps plants make seeds.

Pretty smell is a plant's invitation card.

Not every flower smells sweet to people.

Some smells fit beetles or night moths better.

Sniff gently—do not crush the bloom.

Garden perfume is science plus kindness.

Thank the helpers that answer the scent.

Breathe in soft, smile, leave the flower be.
