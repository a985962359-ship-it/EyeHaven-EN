What Makes an Earthquake?

A storybook floor wiggles softly underfoot.

Earth's outer shell has big pieces called plates.

Plates move very slowly all the time.

Sometimes stuck edges suddenly slip.

That slip sends shaking waves through the ground.

We feel those waves as an earthquake.

Most quakes are small; some are stronger.

Buildings in quake places use special designs.

Drop, cover, and hold on if grown-ups say practice.

Earthquakes are plate motion, not monsters.

Science helps us prepare and stay calmer.

Steady feet, ready plan, soft courage.
