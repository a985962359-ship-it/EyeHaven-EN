Why Do Leaves Change Color?

Autumn trees wear red and gold coats.

Green comes from chlorophyll making food.

In fall, many trees slow that green work.

Chlorophyll fades, and other colors show.

Yellows and oranges were hiding underneath.

Some reds form as sugars change in cool air.

Shorter days signal leaves to prepare.

A gentle leaf scar seals where the leaf lets go.

Fallen leaves can feed the soil later.

Not every tree turns the same rainbow.

Fall color is chemistry wearing a costume.

Catch one leaf—then smile at the season.
