Why Do Oceans Have Tides?

Beach water creeps up the sand, then back.

The Moon's gravity pulls on Earth's oceans.

Water bulges a little toward the Moon.

Another bulge forms on the opposite side too.

As Earth turns, those bulges sweep along shores.

We see high tide and low tide.

The Sun's pull adds a smaller tide effect.

When Sun and Moon line up, tides can be bigger.

Tide pools appear when water goes out.

Check tide times with a grown-up before exploring.

Tides are gravity dancing with spinning Earth.

Wave hello to the Moon's quiet ocean tug.
