Why Do Volcanoes Erupt?

A mountain with a soft puff sits in a drawing.

Deep Earth can be hot enough to melt rock.

Melted rock is called magma.

Magma can find a path upward through cracks.

When it reaches the surface, we call it lava.

Gas bubbles help push magma up sometimes.

An eruption is Earth letting pressure out.

Some eruptions are gentle; some are stronger.

Scientists watch volcanoes to keep people safe.

Never visit a real volcano without grown-ups and rules.

Volcanoes build new land over long time.

Hot rock, rising paths, careful respect.
