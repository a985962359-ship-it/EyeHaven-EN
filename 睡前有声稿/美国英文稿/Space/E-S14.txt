What Is the Milky Way?

A milky band in a dark sky. “Our galaxy?”

The Milky Way is the huge star city we live in—billions of stars, gas, and dust in a flat spiral. From Earth we see it edge-on as a pale river of light.

Other galaxies shine far beyond. Telescopes are our ships.

So the Milky Way isn’t spilled milk—it’s home on a cosmic scale.
