Why Is the Sun So Bright?

Sunshine pours across the room. "Ow—bright!"

The Sun is a star—our closest one.

Inside, hot gases release enormous energy.

Light and warmth race outward in every direction.

Earth catches only a tiny share of that light.

Even a tiny share is enough to light our day.

Never stare straight at the Sun—it can hurt eyes.

Use shade, hats, and sideways glances outdoors.

Sunset glow is sunlight traveling a longer path.

Night comes when our side turns away from the Sun.

Bright does not mean small—our star is huge.

Thank the Sun, then play in safe soft light.
