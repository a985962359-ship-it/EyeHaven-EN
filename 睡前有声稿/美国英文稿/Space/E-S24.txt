What Are Constellations?

Dot-to-dot bears and hunters. “Real groups?”

Constellations are patterns people imagined by connecting stars as seen from Earth. The stars in a pattern may not be close to each other in space—just lined up from our viewpoint.

Cultures drew different sky stories. Astronomers still use the names as maps.

So constellations are sky storytelling overlays on real distant suns.
