Why Do Stars Twinkle?

Stars wink above. "Blinking on purpose?"

Starlight travels a very long way to Earth.

It passes through moving layers of air.

Air bends the light a tiny bit, again and again.

To our eyes, the star seems to dance and wink.

Planets look steadier—they are closer bright disks.

Twinkle is Earth's air, not the star flipping a switch.

On a still clear night, winks can look softer.

City haze may make twinkles look busier.

Astronauts above the air see stars shine steady.

So twinkle means: light plus our restless sky.

Next clear night, watch one star gently wink.
