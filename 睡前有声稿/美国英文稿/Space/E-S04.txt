Why Is There No Sound in Space?

Clap in a pretend spacesuit. Total silence!

Sound needs something to travel through.

Usually that something is air, water, or solid stuff.

Tiny pushes pass along as a wave we hear.

Space between worlds is mostly empty of air.

With almost no air, sound waves have nowhere to go.

That is why space feels so quiet in stories.

Inside a spaceship, air lets voices work again.

On Earth, air is the everyday sound highway.

Put a bell under a jar and pump air out—quieter.

No air highway means no ordinary clap-sound.

Quiet space, noisy Earth—same science, different room.
