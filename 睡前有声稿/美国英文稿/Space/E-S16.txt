What Is a Black Hole?

A dark circle in a diagram. “Scary?”

A black hole is a place where gravity is so strong that light can’t escape from inside a boundary called the event horizon. They form from very massive stars collapsing, among other ways.

They don’t roam eating everything nearby like monsters. Orbits can be stable far out.

So black holes are extreme gravity sinks—studied carefully, not bedtime villains.
