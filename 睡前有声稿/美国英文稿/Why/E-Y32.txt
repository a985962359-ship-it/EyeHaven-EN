Why Do We See Our Breath in Winter?

A steamy puff. “Smoke?!”

Your breath holds water vapor. Cold air cools that vapor into tiny droplets you can see—like a mini cloud. It isn’t smoke or dragons, just warm moist air meeting chill.

Cover your mouth with a scarf if wind bites. Puffs mean winter physics.

So visible breath is a personal weather cloud, gone in a second.
