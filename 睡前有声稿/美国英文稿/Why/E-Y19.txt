Why Does Salt Melt Ice on Sidewalks?

White salt on ice. “How does that work?”

Salt lowers the freezing point of water. Ice can melt even when the air is still a bit below the normal freezing temperature. The salty water stays liquid longer.

Too much salt can bother plants and pets’ paws—grown-ups choose carefully.

So sidewalk salt is chemistry that asks ice to give up sooner.
