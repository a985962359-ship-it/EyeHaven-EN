Why Is the Ocean Salty?

Guangguang tastes pretend seawater and makes a funny face. “Why is the ocean salty?”

Rain falls on rocks and soil. Water carries tiny bits of salt and minerals into rivers, and rivers carry them to the sea. The sun lifts water into clouds, but most salt stays behind. Over a very long time, the ocean collected more salt.

Not every lake is salty—some have rivers that carry salt out. The ocean is big and mostly keeps what arrives.

So ocean salt isn’t magic seasoning. It’s rock bits that traveled with water for ages.
