Why Is the Ocean Salty?

Pretend sip—blech! "Why so salty?"

Rain falls on rocks and soil inland.

Water picks up tiny mineral bits, including salt.

Rivers carry those bits toward the sea.

Ocean water keeps collecting dissolved minerals.

Sun evaporates water, but salt mostly stays behind.

Over long time, seas taste salty.

Not every lake is as salty as the ocean.

Some inland seas can be even saltier.

Salt is a traveler that settled in the big blue.

Waves splash—taste is science, not magic.

Rinse hands after beach play—salt sticks!
