Why Do Hurricanes Spin?

A spinning storm on the map! "Why the swirl?"

Hurricanes are huge storms that form over warm ocean water.

Rising moist air fuels tall storm clouds.

Earth's spin helps steer the storm into a rotating swirl.

That effect is called the Coriolis effect in science class.

Winds circle around a calmer center called the eye.

Hurricane watches mean families prepare early.

Follow grown-up and weather service safety plans.

Never go outside to watch dangerous winds.

Warm water, rising air, Earth spin: swirl recipe.

Respect big storms; learn from safe screens and books.

Spin science keeps us curious and careful.
