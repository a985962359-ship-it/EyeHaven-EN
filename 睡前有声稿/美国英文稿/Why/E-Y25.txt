Why Do We Get Brain Freeze From Cold Drinks?

Ow—forehead zap! “Brain freeze?”

Very cold drinks can chill nerves in the roof of your mouth. Those nerves send a loud pain message that can feel like it’s in your forehead. It usually fades in under a minute.

Warm your tongue on the roof of your mouth. Sip slower next time.

So brain freeze is chilly nerves yelling, not your brain turning into ice.
