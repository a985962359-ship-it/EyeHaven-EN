Why Is the Sky Dark at Night?

Day blue, night black. "Where did the light go?"

At night our side of Earth turns away from the Sun.

Without daytime sunlight, the sky looks dark.

Stars were there all along—sunlight hides them by day.

City lights can brighten the night and hide faint stars.

The Moon reflects sunlight and can glow softly.

Night helps many animals hunt or rest on their schedules.

Sleepy humans use night for rest and dreams.

Look at stars with a grown-up in a safe open place.

Darkness is Earth's night-side view of space.

Turn away from Sun, stars show: night sky science.

Blue day, dark night—one spinning planet.
