Why Does Bread Get Moldy?

Green fuzz on bread. “Ew—why?”

Mold is a tiny living fungus. Spores float in air, land on food, and grow when it’s moist and not too cold. Bread is a cozy buffet for them.

Cool, dry, sealed storage slows mold. Fuzzy bread should be thrown away—don’t just scrape the top.

So mold isn’t magic dirt. It’s life throwing a party on leftovers.
