Why Does the Wind Blow?

Leaves dance. "Who pushes the air?" asks Guangguang.

Sun warms some places more than others.

Warm air rises and cooler air slides in to fill the space.

That moving air is wind we feel on our faces.

Big weather patterns make breezes and stronger gusts.

Mountains and oceans can steer how wind flows.

A gentle breeze helps fly a kite on a safe open day.

Strong storms need grown-up plans—stay inside when told.

Windmills can turn moving air into useful power.

Feel wind safely; never chase storms for fun.

Air is invisible, but wind shows it is busy.

Warm rise, cool slide, moving air: wind science.
