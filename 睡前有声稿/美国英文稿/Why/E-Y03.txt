Why Do Onions Make Us Cry?

Chop chop—then sniffles. Onion tears!

Cutting an onion breaks open tiny cells.

Those cells release sharp-smelling gases.

The gases rise and tickle your eyes.

Eyes make tears to wash the tickle away.

Tears are a helpful rinse, not sadness only.

Chilling an onion can slow the gas a bit.

Cut near a gentle fan blowing gases away.

Grown-ups may cry too—same onion chemistry.

Soup still tastes great after the sniffles.

Science in the kitchen: smell meeting eyes.

Blink, smile, keep cooking with care.
