Why Do Onions Make Us Cry?

Guangguang chops an onion for soup practice—chop chop—then sniffles. “Why do onions make us cry?”

When you cut an onion, it releases a sharp gas. The gas floats up to your eyes. Your eyes make tears to wash the sting away. That is a smart shield, not sadness.

Chilling the onion or cutting near a little airflow can help. You are not “too soft”—your eyes are doing their job.

So onion tears are chemistry meeting your eyelids. Next time, blink, step back, and finish the soup with Guangguang.
