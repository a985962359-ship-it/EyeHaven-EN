What Is Hail?

Ice pellets bounce! "Is hail frozen rain?"

Hail forms in strong thunderstorms with tall cold clouds.

Tiny ice bits get coated with water that freezes again.

Updrafts toss the growing ice balls up and down.

When they grow heavy, they fall as hailstones.

Hail can be pea-sized or sometimes larger.

Car roofs and gardens may need cover during hail.

Stay indoors until the bouncing ice stops.

Hail is not the same as soft winter snowflakes.

Thunderstorm engines can make summer ice.

Up, coat, fall: hail factory science.

Respect hail; watch from a safe window.
