Why Do We See Lightning Before Thunder?

Flash! Then boom. "Why the wait?"

Lightning is a giant spark of electricity in the sky.

Light races to your eyes almost instantly.

Thunder is the sound of air jumping from the heat.

Sound travels much slower than light through air.

So the flash arrives first; the rumble follows.

Counting seconds between flash and boom estimates distance.

Stay safe indoors during thunderstorms.

Never stand under a lone tall tree in a storm.

Lightning is powerful—admire from a safe place.

Light wins the race; sound comes second.

Flash first, boom later: storm timing science.
