Why Do Cats Knead Soft Blankets?

Paws press-press. “Why bakery paws?”

Kneading is common in cats. Kittens press to help milk flow; grown cats may knead when cozy, claiming a soft spot with scent from their paws, or remembering kitten comfort.

It’s usually a compliment to the blanket—and to you.

So kneading is comfort behavior with kitten roots.
