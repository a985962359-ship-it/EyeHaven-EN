Why Does Ice Cream Melt?

A scoop sags. “Why so fast?” Max wails.

Ice cream is frozen water, fat, and sugar mixed with air. Warm air and your tongue add heat. Heat makes ice crystals turn back into liquid, so the scoop collapses into soup.

Shade and a cold bowl slow the melt. Summer sun speeds it up.

So melting is heat winning against frozen crystals—delicious, temporary art.
