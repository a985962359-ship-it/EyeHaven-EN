Why Do We Have Belly Buttons?

Max pokes his. “What’s this scar for?”

Before birth, a cord brought food and oxygen from mom. After birth, the cord is clamped and falls away. The belly button is the healed mark that remains.

Innies and outies are both normal. It’s a souvenir of how you grew.

So a belly button is a tiny life-history badge, not a button for clothes.
