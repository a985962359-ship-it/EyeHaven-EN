Why Do Clouds Look Fluffy?

Cotton candy sky! "Are clouds pillows?"

Clouds are tiny water drops or ice bits floating together.

From far away, the crowd of drops looks soft and fluffy.

Up close they are wet mist, not stuffed toys.

Different cloud shapes form in different air layers.

Fluffy cumulus often grow on fair sunny days.

Gray sheets can mean steady rain is coming.

Wind stretches clouds into streaks across the blue.

Planes fly through clouds—pilots train for that carefully.

Watch clouds from the ground; don't climb toward storms.

Fluffy looks are a crowd of tiny floaters.

Drops, distance, soft look: cloud science.
