What Is Fog?

White morning air! "Is fog a cloud hug?"

Fog is a cloud that forms near the ground.

Tiny water drops hang in cool moist air.

When air cools enough, water vapor becomes visible droplets.

Fog makes lights look soft and fuzzy.

Drivers go slow because seeing far is harder.

Sea coasts and valleys often get foggy mornings.

Sun warmth can lift and clear the fog later.

Walking in fog feels magical but stay with a grown-up.

Fog is weather you can almost touch.

Tiny floaters near the ground make the white veil.

Ground cloud, tiny drops, soft world: fog science.
