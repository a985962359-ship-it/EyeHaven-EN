Why Does Glue Stick?

Two papers hug. “How does glue work?”

Wet glue flows into tiny roughness on surfaces, then hardens or dries. It grabs both sides like a microscopic handshake. Different glues use different chemistry—school glue isn’t the same as wood glue.

Use the right glue for the job, and keep caps closed.

So sticking is chemistry plus grip in the tiny hills of a surface.
