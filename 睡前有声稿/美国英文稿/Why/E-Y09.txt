Why Do We Have Five Fingers?

Guangguang counts Max’s fingers. “Why five?”

Humans usually grow five digits on each hand. That layout helps us grip, pinch, and use tools. Fossils show our distant relatives also had a five-digit pattern that worked well and stuck around.

Some people are born with more or fewer digits. Bodies can vary and still be wonderful.

So five fingers aren’t a rule of the universe—they’re a useful design that evolution kept.
