Why Do Stars Look Pointy in Drawings?

Max draws spikes. “Real stars are round, right?”

Real stars are giant glowing balls. Twinkling and camera effects can make them look pointy to our eyes and in photos. Artists add points so stars pop on paper.

Space telescopes show rounder, sharper views above Earth’s blurry air.

So drawn points are art language; stars themselves are blazing spheres.
