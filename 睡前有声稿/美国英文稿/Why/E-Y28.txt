Why Do We Have Different Blood Types?

Guangguang shows colored stickers. “A, B, O—why?”

People’s red blood cells can carry different marker molecules. We group them into types so doctors match transfusions safely. Your type is inherited from family.

Knowing your type is useful in emergencies. Donating blood helps others—when grown-ups say you’re old enough.

So blood types are name tags on cells, discovered so medicine stays safe.
