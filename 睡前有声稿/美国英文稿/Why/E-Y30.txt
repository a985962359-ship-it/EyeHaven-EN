Why Do We Yawn When Others Yawn?

Guangguang yawns. Max yawns. “Contagious?!”

Yawning can spread among people and some animals. Scientists think it may relate to empathy, cooling the brain, or syncing alertness in a group. We’re still learning.

You can’t always stop a contagious yawn. It’s okay—and kind of funny.

So shared yawns may be social brain echoes, not boredom contests.
