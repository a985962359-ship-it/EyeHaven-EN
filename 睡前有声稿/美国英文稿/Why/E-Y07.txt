Why Does Metal Feel Colder Than Wood?

Guangguang touches a metal spoon and a wooden spoon. “The metal feels colder—but are they different temperatures?”

Often they are nearly the same temperature in the room. Metal moves heat away from your fingers quickly, so your skin cools fast and you feel “cold.” Wood moves heat more slowly, so it feels warmer to the touch.

A winter metal slide can feel icy for the same reason—it steals heat from your hands fast.

So “feels colder” is about heat leaving your skin, not always about the object being frozen.
