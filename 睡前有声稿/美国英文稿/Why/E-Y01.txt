Why Is Grass Green?

Lawn picnic. "Why green, not purple?"

Grass leaves hold a tiny helper called chlorophyll.

Chlorophyll catches sunlight to help make food.

It reflects more green light toward our eyes.

So grass looks green while it works.

Other paints in leaves hide under that green.

In fall, some greens fade and reds show up.

Healthy grass still needs water and light.

Too little sun can make grass look pale.

Green is a working color, not just decoration.

Touch soft blades—tiny solar kitchens.

Green grass means sunlight helpers are busy!
