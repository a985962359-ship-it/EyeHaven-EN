Why Do We Hiccup?

Hic—! Guangguang blinks. “Why do we hiccup?”

A hiccup is a sudden squeeze of the breathing muscle under your lungs, called the diaphragm. Air pops in, and your voice box makes a tiny “hic.” Spicy food, fizzy drinks, or a big laugh can tickle that muscle.

Most hiccups stop on their own. Slow breaths and a sip of water can help. If hiccups last a long time, tell a grown-up.

So hiccups aren’t a tiny animal sneezing inside you. They’re a short glitch in a helpful breathing muscle.
