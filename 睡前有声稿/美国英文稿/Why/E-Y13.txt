Why Do We Get Hiccups After Soda?

Fizz! Hic! “Why now?”

Bubbly drinks can tickle your stomach and the diaphragm—the breathing muscle under your lungs. A sudden squeeze makes that tiny “hic.” Cold fizz and fast gulps make it more likely.

Slow sips help. Most hiccups pass soon.

So soda hiccups are bubbles plus a jumpy breathing muscle.
