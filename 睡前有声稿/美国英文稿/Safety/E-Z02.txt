Look Far, Then Near

Mia's nose nearly touches the tablet. "Too close!"

Eyes work hard when everything stays near.

Looking far lets eye muscles change focus.

Near work is fine in short friendly turns.

Then glance out a window to something distant.

Trees, clouds, or a far wall all help.

Far, then near—like a gentle stretch for eyes.

Guangguang coaches: chin up, soft blinks.

If the screen feels sticky-close, scoot back.

Rest breaks protect comfort, not just rules.

Mia practices: far tree, then tablet, smile.

Eyes happy: look far, then come back near.
