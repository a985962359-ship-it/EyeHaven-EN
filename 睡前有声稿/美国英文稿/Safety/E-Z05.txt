Blink and Sip Water

"When did you last blink?" Max wonders.

Screens can make us forget soft blinks.

Blinking spreads a tear film like a tiny wipe.

Dry scratchy eyes often need more blinks.

Sip water—bodies and eyes like gentle sips.

Set a cozy blink-and-sip habit during play.

Look away, blink slowly three times, smile.

Guangguang counts blinks like a game.

If eyes feel sandy, tell a grown-up.

Comfort drinks and blinks are eye kindness.

Max blinks, sips, and the screen feels softer.

Blink, sip, continue—little care, big comfort.
