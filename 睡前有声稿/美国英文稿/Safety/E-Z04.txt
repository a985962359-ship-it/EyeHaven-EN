Sit Up, Don’t Bend Close

Mia folds like a pretzel over her book.

Bending close makes neck and eyes work harder.

Sit up with soft shoulders and a tall spine.

Bring the book or tablet up—not your nose down.

A pillow or stand can lift the screen kindly.

Feet on the floor help your whole posture.

Short wiggle breaks keep bodies happy.

Guangguang mirrors a proud sitting pose.

If you catch a slump, reset gently—no scolding.

Comfort now means easier reading later.

Sit up, soft eyes, friendly page distance.

Pretzel rest is for snacks—not for screens!
