Why Do Penguins Huddle Together?

Guangguang wraps a scarf around a stuffed penguin. “Why do penguins huddle?”

In icy places, penguins stand close in a big group. Warm bodies share heat, like many little heaters standing shoulder to shoulder. They take turns moving from the cold outside of the huddle to the warmer inside.

Huddling is teamwork, not just cuddling for fun. Each bird helps the others stay warm enough to live.

So a penguin huddle is a living blanket. Next time you see friends stick together in a cold wind, you’re practicing a tiny bit of penguin wisdom.
