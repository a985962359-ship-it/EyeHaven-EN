Why Do Spiders Spin Webs?

A dew-bright web shines. “Why spin webs?” asks Guangguang.

Many spiders make silk from their bodies. A web is a trap and a home base. Sticky threads catch flying snacks; the spider feels the shake and hurries over.

Not every spider builds the same web. Some hunt without big nets. Silk can also wrap eggs or help a spider travel on the breeze.

So a web is engineering with protein thread—quiet, patient, and surprisingly strong.
