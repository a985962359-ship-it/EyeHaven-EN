Why Do Dolphins Click and Whistle?

Clicks echo in Guangguang’s pretend ocean. “Why so noisy?”

Dolphins use sound to talk and to “see.” Clicks bounce off fish and the seafloor; returning echoes paint a sound-picture called echolocation. Whistles help friends stay in touch.

Water carries sound well, so voices travel far. A quiet dolphin would miss dinner and friends.

So dolphin noise is a toolkit: chat plus underwater sonar.
