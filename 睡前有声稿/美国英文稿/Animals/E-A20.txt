Why Do Elephants Have Trunks?

Guangguang tries to pick up a leaf with two fingers. “Elephants use a trunk—why?”

An elephant’s trunk is a long nose and upper lip working together. It can smell, breathe, spray water, and grab food. Soft tips act like gentle fingers for tiny things; strong muscles lift heavier branches.

A trunk helps an elephant drink and cool off, too. It is a tool, a nose, and a hand in one.

So trunks aren’t decoration. They’re Swiss-army noses built for a big, busy life.
