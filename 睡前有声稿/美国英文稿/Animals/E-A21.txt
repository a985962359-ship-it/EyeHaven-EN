Why Do Giraffes Have Long Necks?

Max looks up at a tall tree. “Why are giraffe necks so long?”

A long neck helps a giraffe reach leaves other animals can’t. It also helps them watch for danger across the grass. Extra-strong hearts pump blood all the way up that tall road.

Giraffes still bend carefully to drink. Long necks are useful, but every body has trade-offs.

So a giraffe neck is a leafy ladder and a lookout tower—grown for the savanna, not for looking fancy.
