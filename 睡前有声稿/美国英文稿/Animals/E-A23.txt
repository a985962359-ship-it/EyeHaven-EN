Why Do Bats Sleep Upside Down?

Guangguang hangs a sock puppet by its toes. “Bats sleep upside down—why?”

Bat feet and legs are great at locking onto a perch. Hanging takes little energy once they’re hooked. From that start, they can drop into flight more easily than jumping up from the ground.

Caves and trees give safe, dark rest spots. Upside-down sleeping fits how their bodies work.

So bats aren’t being dramatic. They’re resting in the position that makes waking up into flight simplest.
