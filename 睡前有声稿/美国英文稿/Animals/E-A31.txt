Why Do Hummingbirds Hover?

Wings blur. “How do they hang in the air?” Guangguang asks.

Hummingbirds beat their wings in a special figure-eight path very, very fast. That motion makes lift on the way forward and back, so they can hover like tiny helicopters while sipping nectar.

They need lots of sugary fuel. Hovering costs energy.

So hovering is high-speed wing math—beautiful and hungry.
