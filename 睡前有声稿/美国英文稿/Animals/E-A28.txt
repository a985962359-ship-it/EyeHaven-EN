Why Do Crabs Walk Sideways?

A crab skitters left. “Why sideways?” Guangguang laughs.

Many crabs have joints that swing best to the side. Sideways steps fit their shell and leg design. They can still move forward when they need to, but the sideways shuffle is their specialty.

Hard shells protect them; quick side steps help them dart into holes.

So crab walking matches crab plumbing—legs built for a sideways groove.
