Why Do Kangaroos Hop?

Guangguang bounce-bounces. “Why hop?”

Kangaroos have strong back legs and big feet. Hopping stores and returns energy in tendons, like springy rubber bands. On open ground, hopping can be an efficient way to travel.

A thick tail helps with balance. Joeys ride in a pouch while moms hop along.

So hopping isn’t just play. It’s a springy highway built into kangaroo legs.
