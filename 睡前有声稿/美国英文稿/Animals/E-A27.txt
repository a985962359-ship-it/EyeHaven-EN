Why Do Fireflies Glow?

Tiny lights blink in the grass. “Why glow?” asks Max.

Fireflies make light with a chemical reaction in their bodies—cold light, not a hot flame. The flashes help them find mates. Different patterns can mean different messages.

Kids should watch, not catch too many. Wild lights belong in the night.

So a firefly glow is living Morse code—soft, safe light with a purpose.
