Why Do Polar Bears Have White Fur?

White fur against snow. “Camouflage?” Max asks.

Polar bear fur looks white, which helps them blend with ice and snow while hunting. Underneath, their skin is dark, which can soak up warmth from sunlight. Hollow hairs can also trap air for insulation.

They’re built for cold seas and ice. Climate and sea ice matter for their future.

So “white” is a hunting cloak and a cold-weather suit in one fluffy package.
