Why Do Zebras Have Stripes?

Black and white flash by. “Why stripes?” Max asks.

Scientists study several helpful ideas: stripes may confuse biting flies, blur motion for predators, or help zebras recognize one another in a herd. One zebra’s pattern is like a fingerprint.

Stripes don’t make a zebra invisible in bright sun, but they can still be useful in the wild.

So zebra fashion is survival science—still being studied, already amazing.
