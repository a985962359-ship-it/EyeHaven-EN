Why Do Beavers Build Dams?

Splash! Sticks pile up. “Why build dams?”

Beavers cut trees and pack mud to slow a stream. The pond that forms gives deep water for safety and a wet entrance to their lodge. Wetlands they create help other animals, too.

Beavers are engineers with teeth. Their work changes the neighborhood on purpose.

So a dam isn’t a toy fort. It’s a beaver home-improvement project for water and safety.
