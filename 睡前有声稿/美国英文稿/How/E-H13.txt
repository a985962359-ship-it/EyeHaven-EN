Why Do Fridges Stay Cold Inside?

Cold lettuce. Warm kitchen. “How?”

A fridge pumps heat from inside to the coils on the back or bottom using a special fluid cycle. It doesn’t make “coldness”—it moves heat out and keeps the door sealed.

Stuffing it full without airflow can weaken cooling. Keep the door shut.

So a fridge is a heat-moving machine with a chilly closet.
