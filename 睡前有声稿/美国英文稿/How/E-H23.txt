Why Do Fans Cool Us If They Don’t Make Cold Air?

Whirr. “Air isn’t icy—why cooler?”

Fans move air across your skin so sweat evaporates faster, carrying heat away. The air temperature may be the same; you feel cooler because heat leaves your body quicker.

In a sealed empty room with no people, a fan mostly just stirs air.

So fan “cool” is evaporating sweat express, not a fridge wind.
