Why Do Boats Float?

A toy boat sits high. “Heavy boats float—how?”

Floating happens when water pushes up with a force equal to the weight of the water the boat pushes aside. A boat’s shape spreads weight over enough water. A solid steel cube may sink; a steel boat hull can float.

Holes that flood the hull spoil the trick. Life jackets help people float too.

So floating is a push-up contest between object and water.
