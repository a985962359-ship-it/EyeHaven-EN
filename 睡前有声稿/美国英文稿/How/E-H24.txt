Why Do Velcro Stick and Peel?

Rrrrip. “How?”

Velcro pairs tiny hooks with tiny loops. Pressing hooks catch loops; peeling unhooks them with that famous sound. Inspired by burrs stuck on clothes after a walk.

Dirt fills loops and weakens grip—clean when fuzzy fails.

So Velcro is miniature grappling hooks taught by nature’s burrs.
