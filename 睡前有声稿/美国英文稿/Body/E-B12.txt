Why Do Muscles Get Sore After Exercise?

Max’s legs ache after soccer. “Why sore?”

Hard work can cause tiny stress in muscle fibers. Your body repairs them a bit stronger. Soreness can peak a day later. Rest, water, and gentle movement help.

Sharp pain or swelling needs a grown-up’s check. Soreness isn’t the same as injury.

So post-workout ouch can be repair day—your muscles remodeling.
