Why Do We Have Skin?

Guangguang pats his smooth shell. “People have skin—why?”

Skin is your largest organ. It keeps insides in, germs out, and senses touch, heat, and pain. It helps control temperature with sweat and blood flow near the surface.

Cuts need gentle cleaning. Sunscreen protects from strong sun.

So skin is armor, sensor, and climate control wrapped around you.
