Why Does My Stomach Growl?

Rumble! "Is a monster in there?" Guangguang asks.

Your stomach and intestines mix food with soft squeezes.

When empty, gas and juices slosh and make growly sounds.

Hunger hormones tell your brain it may be meal time.

Growls can also happen while digesting a big snack.

Drinking water and waiting a bit often settles the noise.

A balanced meal gives steady fuel instead of big crashes.

Everyone's tummy talks sometimes—it is not a failure.

If pain comes with growls, tell a grown-up.

Rest and calm breathing help when the rumble feels loud.

Your body is a busy kitchen, not a scary cave.

Mix, slosh, signal: tummy soundtrack science.
