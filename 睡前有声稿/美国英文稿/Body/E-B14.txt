Why Does My Stomach Growl?

Grr-owl. Max blushes. “Why so loud?”

Your digestive tube squeezes to move food and gas along. When it’s emptier, the squeezes and air make louder gurgles. Hunger hormones can turn up the activity.

A snack can quiet it. Growls are normal, not rude on purpose.

So tummy thunder is plumbing plus air—sometimes on speakerphone.
