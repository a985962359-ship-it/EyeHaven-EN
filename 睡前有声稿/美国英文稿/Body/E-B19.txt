Why Do We Have Earwax?

Guangguang pretends to clean tiny ears—gently. “Wax?”

Earwax traps dust and bugs, and it helps keep the canal from getting too dry. It slowly works its way out. Cotton swabs can push wax deeper—ask a grown-up about safe cleaning.

A little wax is healthy armor.

So earwax is sticky security, not dirt you must dig out every day.
