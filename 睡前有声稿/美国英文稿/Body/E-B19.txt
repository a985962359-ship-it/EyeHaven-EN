Why Do We Have Earwax?

"Sticky ear stuff—yuck. Why?" Guangguang peeks carefully.

Earwax is a natural cleaner and protector for the ear canal.

It traps dust and tiny bits before they go deeper.

It also helps keep the canal from getting too dry.

Old wax usually works its way out on its own.

Cotton swabs can push wax farther in—skip the dig.

A grown-up or doctor can help if hearing feels blocked.

Never pour random liquids into ears for fun.

Ears do important listening work every day.

Gentle outer cleaning of the ear flap is enough at home.

Wax is a tiny bodyguard, not dirt to dig out.

Trap, protect, migrate out: earwax jobs.
