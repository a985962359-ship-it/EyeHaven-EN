Why Do We Get Chickenpox Only Once Usually?

Itchy dots! "Will it come back forever?" asks Guangguang.

Chickenpox is usually caused by a virus called varicella.

Your immune system learns the virus's face during illness.

Memory cells keep that lesson ready for later.

If the virus visits again, defenses often stop a full round.

That is why most people get the classic rash only once.

Vaccines teach the same lesson more gently beforehand.

Some viruses hide and can return as different itchy shingles later in life.

For kids, rest, soft clothes, and grown-up care help.

Do not scratch hard—nails can open skin to other germs.

Never share lotion guesses; ask a grown-up or doctor.

Learn once, remember long: immune school for pox.
