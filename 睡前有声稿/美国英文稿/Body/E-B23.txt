Why Do Kids Grow So Fast Sometimes?

Pants suddenly short! "Did I stretch overnight?"

Kids grow in spurts when hormones and cells speed up.

Bones lengthen at growth plates near the ends.

Sleep is a big helper—repair and growth signals rise at night.

Food and protein give building blocks for new tissue.

Not every month looks the same; charts zig and zag.

Friends may grow at different times—that is normal.

Clothes and shoes get replaced as the body upgrades.

Gentle stretch and play keep growing bodies happy.

If growth worries you, a grown-up or doctor can check.

Fast seasons of growing are part of becoming you.

Spurts, sleep, fuel, plates: growth science.
