Why Do Babies Have Soft Spots on Their Heads?

Gently—Guangguang shows a doll. “Soft spot?”

Babies’ skull bones aren’t fully fused. Soft spots (fontanelles) let the head pass through birth and give the brain room to grow. They usually close as bones meet over months and years.

Be gentle. Soft doesn’t mean fragile if handled with care.

So soft spots are growth panels on a brand-new skull.
