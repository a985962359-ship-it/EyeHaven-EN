Why Does My Heart Beat Faster When I Run?

After a race, thump-thump! "Why so fast?"

Muscles need more oxygen and fuel during hard play.

Your heart pumps blood that carries those supplies.

Faster beats push more blood each minute.

Lungs work harder too, so breath may get quicker.

When you rest, the beat slows toward normal again.

A strong heart likes regular movement and sleep.

Warm up and cool down help the change feel smoother.

If chest pain or dizziness happens, stop and tell a grown-up.

Feeling your pulse on a wrist can be a calm science check.

Fast beat is your body answering the run.

Demand up, pump up, then settle: heart pacing.
