Why Do We Get Allergies?

Achoo near cats! "Why does my body overreact?"

An allergy is when the immune system treats a harmless thing as a threat.

Pollen, some foods, or pet dander can be triggers for some people.

The body releases chemicals that cause itch, sneeze, or swell.

Not everyone reacts to the same triggers.

Avoiding known triggers and plans from doctors help a lot.

Allergy is different from a cold germ infection.

If breathing gets hard, get grown-up help right away.

Labels on food matter when allergies are serious.

You did nothing wrong—bodies just have different alarm settings.

Learn your triggers and keep calm helpers nearby.
