Why Do Fingernails Grow?

Clip time! "Why do nails keep coming back?"

Nails grow from a root area under the skin at the base.

New cells push older cells forward into the hard plate.

The hard part is mostly keratin, a tough protein.

Nails protect fingertips and help tiny pick-up jobs.

Growth is slow and steady for most kids.

Biting nails can hurt the skin around them.

Keep nails clean and trimmed with grown-up help.

A balanced diet supports many growing body parts, including nails.

If a nail looks painful or infected, tell a grown-up.

Nails are tools and shields that quietly renew.

Root push, keratin plate, protect: nail science.
